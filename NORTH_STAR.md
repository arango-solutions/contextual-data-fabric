# North Star

> **Every agent in the enterprise consults Arango first as its governed,
> semantically-normalized brain — one ontology that spans all systems, answers
> or routes any question with a cited retrieval path, enforces access and
> business rules in one place, and evolves itself as the data changes —
> without moving the data.**

The end state, in six lines:

1. **One ontology, many sources** — new sources are absorbed by extracting and
   aligning their local ontology, never by hand-modeling or boiling the ocean.
2. **Ask anything, in English, across everything** — the ontology decomposes
   the question, the systems of record answer it natively, results reassemble;
   the data never moves.
3. **Every answer is grounded** — the actual SQL/AQL and source objects ride
   with every answer, and the system refuses rather than guesses.
4. **Governance lives in the ontology** — a rule declared once (PII, access,
   business logic) takes effect across every source and every agent.
5. **The ontology is alive** — schema and document changes cascade through
   belief management, versioned and change-controlled.
6. **Composable, not monolithic** — customers adopt the blocks they need.
